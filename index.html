Who We Are

Healing Streams Care Services, LLC is licensed to deliver Home and Community-Based Services in Minnesota for adults and people with disabilities. Our professionals assist individuals eligible for Minnesota's Waiver Programs so they can be catered to under any of the services we provide based on their needs.

We value working closely with individuals and families every step of the way from assessing their needs to providing the necessary supports and meeting agreed-on outcomes so they can live happily, comfortably and independently in their community.

Our professionals are dedicated to helping them develop self-sufficiency and living life to the fullest.
